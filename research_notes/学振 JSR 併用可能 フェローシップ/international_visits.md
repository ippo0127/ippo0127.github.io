# International (non-Japanese) programs for research visits, schools and visiting-graduate-student positions — compatibility with JSPS DC + UTokyo JSR

Scope: hep-th / mathematical-physics PhD student, Japanese national, enrolled at UTokyo (Kavli IPMU). Checked on 2026-11-12. "Compatible / conditional / incompatible" is my classification relative to keeping DC + JSR (the JSPS/JSR rules themselves are not re-verified here; see Inferences).

## Q1. Institute visiting programs for PhD students (stipend / housing / travel)

### Takeaway
The best-funded open-to-international PhD visiting programs are the KITP Graduate Fellowship (5–6 months, housing + up to $1200/month local expenses + travel help), Mittag-Leffler junior fellowships (full 3.5-month semester, monthly grant + free housing + travel), HIM Bonn trimester programs (housing + travel allowance for ≥4-week PhD stays), and the Nordita Visiting PhD Fellow program (1–2 months housing + travel up to 4000 SEK, no salary). None of the official pages read contains a "no other stipend" clause; most only cover local costs, so they are compatible in practice. Perimeter, CERN TH, SCGP, IHES, Harvard CMSA and NCTS have no funded PhD-visitor program — visits are host-invited and self-funded (CERN TH explicitly cannot fund PhD students).

### Cited Findings

**KITP Graduate Fellowship Program (UCSB)** — compatible (no other-funding rule found)
- Open to "advanced physics doctoral students from around the world"; no nationality or home-institution restriction stated; aimed at students near the end of their PhD — [KITP](https://www.kitp.ucsb.edu/apply/fellowships/graduate-fellowship-program)
- Stay "minimum period of 5 months (and up to 6 months)"; fall term (Jul–Dec) or spring term (Jan–Jun); up to eight fellows at a time; fellows expected to stay the whole period — [KITP](https://www.kitp.ucsb.edu/apply/fellowships/graduate-fellowship-program)
- Coverage: "reimbursement for local expenses (meals and incidentals) up to a maximum of $1200/month"; "Campus housing will also be provided"; KITP assists with round-trip travel between home institution and KITP — [KITP](https://www.kitp.ucsb.edu/apply/fellowships/graduate-fellowship-program)
- Apply directly or by advisor nomination; CV + 500-word essay + advisor letter. Deadlines: winter/spring term by **September 15 of the previous fall**; summer/fall term by **February 15 of that year** → next realistic: **15 Feb 2027** (for Jul–Dec 2027), then 15 Sep 2027 (for Jan–Jun 2028) — [KITP](https://www.kitp.ucsb.edu/apply/fellowships/graduate-fellowship-program)
- The page contains no rule about other funding — [KITP](https://www.kitp.ucsb.edu/apply/fellowships/graduate-fellowship-program)
- An older third-party notice said students could not apply directly (nomination only); the current KITP page allows both — [search summary of Illinois/Hispanic Outlook notices](https://physics.illinois.edu/academics/graduates/blog/41518) vs [KITP](https://www.kitp.ucsb.edu/apply/fellowships/graduate-fellowship-program)

**Institut Mittag-Leffler (Djursholm, Sweden) junior fellowships** — compatible (no rule found), but topic must match the semester program
- Eligibility: "either an advanced graduate student or a recent PhD (degree within 5 years before the start of research program)"; research must fall within the program area; no nationality rule stated — [Mittag-Leffler call](https://www.mittag-leffler.se/calls/call-for-junior-fellowships/)
- Fellowship is for the full program (3.5 months); covers a monthly grant (amount not stated on page), free accommodation at IML, and one return trip — [Mittag-Leffler call](https://www.mittag-leffler.se/calls/call-for-junior-fellowships/)
- Currently open call: Spring 2028 "Subelliptic and Magnetic Operators and Their Interactions" (9 Feb–19 May 2028), **deadline 15 Nov 2026** (off-topic for TQFT) — [Mittag-Leffler call](https://www.mittag-leffler.se/calls/call-for-junior-fellowships/)
- Relevant 2027 program: "Quantum Fields, Probability, and Geometry", 10 Feb–21 May 2027 (rigorous QFT via probability and geometry); no open junior-fellow call for it found (deadline presumably passed); fall 2027 program "Triangulated Categories in Algebra, Combinatorics, Geometry, and Topology" (1 Sep–10 Dec 2027) — [Mittag-Leffler program page](https://www.mittag-leffler.se/activities/quantum-fields-probability-and-geometry); [Mittag-Leffler home](https://www.mittag-leffler.se)
- IML posts a scam warning: it never asks for payments for accommodation/travel — [Mittag-Leffler](https://www.mittag-leffler.se/activities/quantum-fields-probability-and-geometry)

**Nordita Visiting PhD Fellow Program (Stockholm)** — compatible (explicitly encourages extra external funding)
- Applicant "must be registered elsewhere as a PhD student" in a Nordita research area; must be hosted by Nordita faculty/postdoc who is not the applicant's PhD (co)advisor; project must go beyond the current thesis topic; no nationality rule on the current page (older materials prioritised Nordic/Baltic students) — [Nordita](https://nordita.org/education/visiting-phd-fellow-program/)
- Duration 1–2 months; covers accommodation in Stockholm and "travel expenses up to 4000 SEK"; "Salary, fellowship or per diem are not provided" — [Nordita](https://nordita.org/education/visiting-phd-fellow-program/)
- "The applicants are encouraged to seek for extra external co-funding in order to extend their visit." — [Nordita](https://nordita.org/education/visiting-phd-fellow-program/)
- Deadlines twice a year (~10 May for Jul–Dec visits, ~10 Nov for Jan–Jun visits; page header says "May 20 2026, and Nov 10 2026" — internally inconsistent). The Nov 2026 round has just closed → next: **~10–20 May 2027** — [Nordita](https://nordita.org/education/visiting-phd-fellow-program/)

**Hausdorff Research Institute for Mathematics (HIM, Bonn) trimester programs** — compatible (no rule found)
- PhD students invited to HIM Trimester Programs are offered accommodation and a travel-cost allowance depending on length of stay; a 2027 program page says travel support only for PhD stays of four weeks or longer; long-term guests receive a living-expenses fellowship (thresholds of 15 days or 3 weeks quoted on different pages) — [HIM about](https://mathematics.uni-bonn.de/him/about-the-institute); [HIM 2027 trimester](https://math-events.uni-bonn.de/event/409/abstracts/)
- Upcoming: May 10–Aug 6, 2027 trimester; Junior Trimester Program Jan 10–Apr 7, 2028 (aimed at PhD students/postdocs within six years of PhD) — [HIM 2027](https://math-events.uni-bonn.de/event/409/abstracts/); [HIM JTP 2028](https://math-events.uni-bonn.de/event/1432/)

**Erwin Schrödinger Institute (ESI, Vienna) Junior Research Fellows** — conditional / unclear funding
- Per a University of Vienna project-service notice (not the ESI page): for graduate students and postdocs ≤5 years post-PhD; 1–4 month stays; projects in mathematics or physics linked to Vienna research or an ESI thematic programme; deadlines **March 15, June 15, October 15**; ineligible if employed at a Vienna-area institution in the prior 2 years; described as "without salary from the University of Vienna" — [Univie project service (via search; page now 404)](https://projektservice-mathematik.univie.ac.at/news/news/call-esi-junior-research-fellows/)
- Next deadline: **15 March 2027**.

**Perimeter Institute (Waterloo)** — compatible (self-funded; requires own funding)
- No "Visiting Graduate Fellows" program found on PI's site; graduate students enrolled elsewhere visit via the Visiting Student program with an invitation from a PI researcher; ≥4 weeks' notice — [PI visiting students](https://perimeterinstitute.ca/training/visiting-students)
- PI does not pay stipend or travel: up to 30 days in a PI apartment room "charged at $30 CDN per day"; students arrange own flights and insurance; "Please ensure you have adequate funding for your visit" (estimated CAD 1,800–2,200/month) — [PI visiting students](https://perimeterinstitute.ca/training/visiting-students)
- Students funded by home institution may count as "Self Funded Researchers" (work-permit exempt) — [PI visiting students](https://perimeterinstitute.ca/training/visiting-students)

**CERN Theory Department** — compatible but unfunded
- "By Council, CERN Theory is not allowed to support PhD students"; short-term visits (1 week–3 months) for PhD students follow standard short-term rules, 4–6 weeks review; visitors need own health/accident insurance — [CERN TH students](https://th-dep.web.cern.ch/students); [CERN TH visitor info](https://theory.cern/node/76)

**Simons Center for Geometry and Physics (Stony Brook)** — compatible
- All visitors must be affiliated with or enrolled at a recognized institution; most visits tied to the 4–6 programs per year (1 month–semester); few general visits — [SCGP visitor info](https://scgp.stonybrook.edu/visitor-info); [SCGP general visits](https://scgp.stonybrook.edu/science/general-visits)
- No dedicated funded graduate-visitor scheme found; graduate students apply to individual events via the event application form — [SCGP event application](https://scgp.stonybrook.edu/science/apply/event)

**IHES (Bures-sur-Yvette)** — no PhD visitor route found
- Visitor applications examined by the Scientific Council in June and December; invited researchers get per diem, office and free residence accommodation; ~a dozen PhD students hosted are mostly Paris-Saclay students; summer schools for ~100 PhD students/postdocs — [IHES PhD students & postdocs](https://www.ihes.fr/en/scholars/phdstudents-postdocs/); [IHES applications](https://www.ihes.fr/?p=1942)

**Max Planck Institute for Mathematics (Bonn)** — likely postdoc-level
- Guest program selects twice a year on individual excellence, "stipends are commensurate with experience"; ~700 visitors/year; no PhD-visitor stipend figure found — [MPIM guest program](https://www.mpim-bonn.mpg.de/node/76)

**Harvard CMSA** — no graduate-visitor program; Harvard GSAS Visiting Students Program (visiting fellows, term or year, applications twice a year) is a general route — [CMSA opportunities](https://cmsa.fas.harvard.edu/opportunities); [Harvard GSAS VSP](https://gsas.harvard.edu/office/visiting-students-program-office)

**NCTS Physics (Taiwan)** — host-funded visits only; visitor for research collaboration "supported from general operation budget of each group" — [NCTS visitor form](https://www.phys.ncts.ntu.edu.tw/uploads/asset/data/63b8cba01d41c8f2230a6ff1/NCTS-phys-visitor.pdf)

**ICTP (Trieste) STEP / HECAP short visits** — incompatible by eligibility (not funding)
- STEP sandwich fellowships are for PhD students from developing countries (3–6 months/year at ICTP); HECAP short-term visits are for scientists from/working in developing countries — [ICTP STEP](https://portal.ictp.it/step); [ICTP HECAP 2026 visits](https://www.ictp.it/opportunity/hecap-call-2026-short-term-visits)

### Inferences
- KITP Graduate Fellowship is the closest match to a "visiting graduate fellow" position for this profile (hep-th, international eligibility, 5–6 months, local costs only). Its $1200/month is expense reimbursement, not salary, so it should not conflict with DC's prohibition on remuneration; still report to JSPS as outside funding and as a long overseas stay.
- Mittag-Leffler and HIM are worth watching for program topics overlapping TQFT/QFT-geometry; the 2027 Mittag-Leffler QFT semester appears to have missed the junior-fellow window.
- Nordita is a low-friction 1–2 month option (next deadline ~May 2027) and explicitly welcomes co-funding.

### Gaps
- Mittag-Leffler monthly grant amount (SEK) not shown on current call page; old aggregator figures not verified.
- ESI JRF: official ESI page not reachable; whether a monthly stipend is paid (vs. housing only) unverified; the only source is a now-404 Univie notice.
- KIAS, APCTP, Tsinghua/YMSC, Stony Brook YITP, PCTS, DESY theory, AEI/MPP visiting-student schemes: no program page found in this pass — appear to be host-invited visits without a standing PhD-visitor program.
- MITP: programs (up to 4 weeks, ≤25 participants, ≥2 weeks stay) cover local housing for accepted participants ([MITP](https://www.mitp.uni-mainz.de/?p=11895)), but no explicit PhD eligibility rule found.
- Perimeter "Visiting Graduate Fellows": no current program found — treat as discontinued/nonexistent unless PI confirms.

## Q2. Schools and workshops (local-cost support)

### Takeaway
Schools typically cover local costs only (or charge fees), so they are compatible with DC/JSR; the main question is eligibility. Aspen summer is postdoc-level only (grad students only in winter conferences); SLMath summer graduate schools require nomination by an academic sponsor or a US math department; Les Houches charges fees (~€1,100–1,300); PiTP and TASI subsidise local costs.

### Cited Findings
- **Aspen Center for Physics**: summer program open "at the postdoctoral level and above" (graduating PhD students only in exceptional circumstances); winter program accepts graduate students; NSF housing stipend given without application; extra need-based aid limited, priority to stays ≥2 weeks; 2026 summer deadline was Jan 15 — [ACP admissions](https://aspenphys.org/admissions-and-eligibility-criteria/); [ACP financial support](https://aspenphys.org/summer/financial-support/)
- **PiTP (IAS Princeton)**: two-week summer school for advanced grad students and postdocs; 2026 had 77 participants from 28 countries; 2024 deadline April 1; 2027 page not yet found — [IAS PiTP](https://ias.edu/pitp); [PiTP 2024](https://www.ias.edu/node/292966)
- **TASI (Boulder)**: "typically provides some subsidy for student expenses", students likely need partial outside support especially for travel; 2026 deadline March 2, 2026; no 2027 page yet — [CU TASI](https://www.colorado.edu/physics/TASI)
- **SLMath summer graduate schools**: grad students from SLMath Academic Sponsoring Institutions or any US math department are eligible; sponsors nominate up to 4 students/year; SLMath covers local expenses and partial travel; NCTS (Taiwan) is a full sponsor with internal deadline Nov 5, 2026 — [UCSC SLMath summer schools](https://sites.google.com/ucsc.edu/grad-math/opportunities/slmath-summer-schools); [NCTS SLMath notice](https://ncts.ntu.edu.tw/news_detail.php?nid=241)
- **Les Houches**: e.g. Jan 11–22, 2027 school fee €1,100 (deadline 30 Aug 2026); a 2025 school listed PhD/postdoc fee €1,300 — [Indico Les Houches 2027](https://indico.cern.ch/event/1553747/registrations/); [Indico 2025 fees](https://indico.in2p3.fr/event/34495/page/4291-conference-fees)
- **GGI (Florence)**: recurring PhD schools — "GGI Lectures on the Theory of Fundamental Interactions" (Jan 2024) and annual three-week "Advanced Lectures on Fields and Strings"; 2027 dates not found — [GGI Lectures 2024 poster](https://listserv.vub.be/mailman/hyperkitty/list/hep-pheno@listserv.vub.be/message/MKFJCHQJIKYCEFWTX7HXG2B3I4VCLCIK/attachment/4/Poster_GGI_LECTURES_2024.pdf); [Florence string group](https://sites.google.com/view/stringtheoryflorence/activities)
- **Isaac Newton Institute**: all programmes have funds for visits of a few days to a couple of months for UK and overseas participants; organisers decide on daily allowances; contact organisers — [INI how to participate](https://www.newton.ac.uk/news/events/how-to-participate)
- **Simons Collaboration on Global Categorical Symmetries** (directly on-topic): 4th GCS summer school, IHP Paris, June 15–July 3, 2026, invited PhD students/postdocs with "support available for those who apply" (past) — [Indico CNRS](https://indico.math.cnrs.fr/event/14721/); [SCGCS](https://scgcs.berkeley.edu/annual-meeting)
- **Bernoulli Center (EPFL)**: no PhD funding scheme; some schools jointly funded with CIMPA for young researchers — [Bernoulli programs](https://bernoulli.epfl.ch/bernoulli-programs)
- **IHES summer schools**: for ~100 PhD students/postdocs — [IHES](https://www.ihes.fr/en/scholars/phdstudents-postdocs/)

### Inferences
- For TQFT/categorical-symmetry topics, watch the GCS summer school (annual), PiTP (topic varies), GGI Fields & Strings, SCGP programs and HIM trimesters.
- SLMath: UTokyo is probably not an academic sponsor (not verified); access would require a sponsor's nomination.

### Gaps
- 2027 dates/deadlines for PiTP, TASI, GGI, Cargèse, ICTP Spring School on Superstrings, Clay summer school, BIRS not verified in this pass.
- Whether any Japanese institution is an SLMath academic sponsor: not found.

## Q3. Government / bilateral scholarships for Japanese PhD students (DAAD, Swiss ESKAS, Fulbright, France BFE, Erasmus+)

### Takeaway
These are full stipends and most interact with DC: DAAD explicitly offsets a second home-country scholarship above €603/month (conditional — DAAD amount is reduced, not forbidden); Swiss ESKAS research fellowship is a fixed 12-month stay (deadline 30 Nov 2026 for Japanese nationals) with no concurrent-funding rule found; Fulbright dissertation research (6–10 months) and French BFE had no verifiable concurrency rule in accessible text. These are heavy "conditional" options, mostly sensible only for long stays.

### Cited Findings

**DAAD Research Grants – doctoral candidates (研究奨学金)** — conditional (DAAD stipend reduced by DC above threshold)
- For people "doing your doctorate at a university or research institution outside Germany"; doctorate must have started ≤3 years before deadline; no nationality requirement stated; doctoral stays **2–12 months**; €1,400/month (+€460 research allowance if >6 months); travel allowance (application required if >6 months); insurance contributions — [DAAD database 57742121](https://www2.daad.de/deutschland/stipendium/datenbank/en/21148-scholarship-database/?status=4&origin=31&subjectGrps=&daad=&intention=&q=&page=1&detail=57742121)
- Deadlines: 7 October (start ≥May) and 18 March (start ≥July); DAAD Japan lists 2027-cycle deadlines **7 Oct 2026** and **18 Mar 2027**; Japanese application form must also be uploaded — [DAAD Japan scholarships](https://www.daad.jp/ja/find-funding/scholarships/)
- "Benefits from third parties are partially credited towards the DAAD scholarship" — [DAAD database](https://www2.daad.de/deutschland/stipendium/datenbank/en/21148-scholarship-database/?status=4&origin=31&subjectGrps=&daad=&intention=&q=&page=1&detail=57742121)
- F.6: "A second scholarship from an international provider of up to EUR 603 will not have an effect on your DAAD scholarship"; excess is deducted. F.5: other income over €603 gross/month deducted. Funding from another German public body cannot be combined — [DAAD important information](https://www.daad.de/en/studying-in-germany/scholarships/information-for-scholarship-applicants/)
- DAAD Japan advises Japanese PhD candidates planning ≥7 months in Germany to consider the bi-nationally supervised doctoral track (7–24 months; deadline 28 Oct 2026) — [DAAD Japan](https://www.daad.jp/ja/find-funding/scholarships/)

**Swiss Government Excellence Scholarships – Research Fellowship** — conditional (no concurrency rule found; fixed 12 months)
- For Japanese applicants: CHF 2,450/month (CHF 29,400/year), September 2027–August 2028, "Research Fellowship scholarships are not prolongable"; must find host institution + supervisor; **deadline 30 November 2026 for Japanese nationals**, via JASSO — [Swissnex Tokyo ESKAS](https://swissnex.org/about-us/locations/tokyo/academia/eskas/)
- No postdoctoral scholarships for 2027–2028; research fellowships are for foreign doctoral students doing a research stay in Switzerland — [SBFI ESKAS](https://www.sbfi.admin.ch/sbfi/en/home/education/scholarships-and-grants/swiss-government-excellence-scholarships.html)

**Fulbright Japan 大学院博士論文研究プログラム** — conditional (concurrency unverified; next round 2028年度 not yet announced)
- Eligibility: ≥1 year in a Japanese doctoral program as of 1 April 2027 (or 満期退学); excludes those who already have or will obtain the PhD before/during the stay; priority to those with little US residence; 6–10 months; start Fall 2027 or Spring 2028 (2027年度 round) — [Fulbright Japan](https://www.fulbright.jp/scholarship/programs/monogragh.html)
- 2027年度 round: online registration 1 Mar–1 May 2026, documents due 1 July 2026; TOEFL iBT 80+/IELTS 6.0+; ~20 awards across programs — [Fulbright Japan](https://www.fulbright.jp/scholarship/programs/monogragh.html)
- Since 2026年度, tuition for the graduate study and dissertation research programs is fully covered (no cap) — [Fulbright Japan news PDF](https://fulbright.jp/news/2025/dgtgdp00000009by-att/dgtgdp00000009el.pdf)
- FAQ: employment in the US is not possible; 2-year home-country residence obligation applies (J-1) — [Fulbright FAQ](https://www.fulbright.jp/scholarship/application/faq.html)

**French Government Scholarship (BFE) – doctoral** — likely incompatible for pure visits
- 2027–2028 rules (Japanese nationals/permanent residents; doctoral and postdoc): standard type €1,850/month for full PhD in France; co-supervision (cotutelle/codirection) type covers only stays in the agreement, total ≤18 months; "学位取得を伴わない留学は対象外" (non-degree study excluded) — [Tsukuba copy of BFE 2027–2028 rules](https://ssc.sec.tsukuba.ac.jp/content/uploads/sites/37/2026/08/reglement-bfe-2027-2028-jp_vf.pdf); [BFE FAQ 2027](https://ssc.sec.tsukuba.ac.jp/content/uploads/sites/37/2026/08/faq-bfe-2027_vf_0.pdf) (contents via search snippet; PDFs not machine-readable here)

**Erasmus+ International Credit Mobility** — conditional on an institutional agreement
- Covers doctoral level; applicant must be enrolled/employed at an HEI with an Erasmus+ ICM partnership with a European university; UTokyo appeared as partner in a 2017–2019 Trento project (historical only) — [Erasmus Generation ICM](https://erasmusgeneration.org/mobility/erasmus-international-credit-mobility); [Trento ICM](https://international.unitn.it/node/1733)

**Chateaubriand** — US-only (not applicable to Japan-enrolled students; not re-verified).

### Inferences
- DAAD: with DC ≈ 200,000 JPY/month (several hundred euros above €603 at any plausible 2026 rate), DAAD's €1,400 would be reduced by the excess — still net positive but smaller. Whether JSR also counts as a "second scholarship" is unclear.
- Swiss ESKAS, Fulbright and BFE all imply long stays (6–12+ months), which trigger JSPS long-stay reporting and possibly UTokyo/JSR residence conditions; these are the riskiest for keeping DC + JSR and need explicit confirmation from JSPS and the JSR office before applying.
- Practical priority for this profile: KITP Graduate Fellowship (Feb 15, 2027), Nordita (May 2027), DAAD research grant (Mar 18, 2027, 2–12 months in Germany e.g. at HIM/MPIM/AEI/DESY), plus local-cost-only schools/programs.

### Gaps
- Concurrency rules for Swiss ESKAS, Fulbright Japan and BFE with JSPS DC: not found in accessible official text (BFE PDFs not readable without pdftotext; the Tohoku page mentions a "重複受給" section but the conclusion was truncated: [Tohoku PDF](https://www.is.tohoku.ac.jp/media/files/_u/topic/file2/fu_20250827_2.pdf)).
- Fulbright 2028年度 schedule not yet published (likely spring 2027 registration by analogy — unconfirmed).
- DAAD short-term (1–6 months) program for doctoral candidates appears merged into the 2–12 month Research Grants; no separate 2027 short-term call found.
- Japan–Germany/Japan–France bilateral student exchange schemes (other than DAAD/BFE) and Simons Foundation student travel funds: nothing specific found.
