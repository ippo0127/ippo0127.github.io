Proposition 4.24 — proof movie

Proposition 4.24 of Orii–Tachikawa, Möbius fusion algebra: for \(X\in\Fix(\sP)\), \(\Cas(\mathrm{M}(X))=\sum_{Y\in\Irr(\cC)}\varepsilon\bigl(\mathrm{M}(X)\,\mathrm{L}(Y^\vee)\bigr)\,\mathrm{L}(Y)\). The movie follows the paper's proof in three acts, one for each step. Step 1 writes \(\Cas(\mathrm{M}(X))\) as a sum of single Möbius diagrams in which an \(\Omega\)-loop encircles two strands. Step 2 expands this loop, moves part of the picture once around the core and cuts the loop open, so that each term becomes a closed picture times \(\mathrm{L}(Y)\). Step 3 identifies the closed picture with \(\Tr(R_{\xi,b_Y})=\varepsilon\bigl(\mathrm{M}(X)\,\mathrm{L}(Y^\vee)\bigr)\). Numbering follows the version of the paper of 18 November 2026.
