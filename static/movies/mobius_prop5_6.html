Proposition 5.6 — proof movie

Proposition 5.6 of Orii–Tachikawa, Möbius fusion algebra, states that \(\sum_{E\in\Irr(\cC)}d_E\Tr_{\cH_E}(w\mapsto u\cdot w\cdot v)=D^2\,\varepsilon(u)\,\varepsilon(v)\) for all \(u,v\in\fX\). The movie follows its proof for the basis elements \(u=\mathrm{M}(A)\), \(v=\mathrm{M}(B)\), one act per step. Step 1 writes the left-hand side as a sum of closed diagrams and removes the sum over \(E\) with (E3). Step 2 turns the sum over \(Y\) into an \(\Omega\)-loop around \(A\), which Lemma 4.5 evaluates.
